# Exposure requirements

The capitalized key word MUST follows [BCP 14](https://www.rfc-editor.org/info/bcp14).

<a id="qual-req-018"></a>
## QUAL-REQ-018 Certain forbidden exposure

A forbidden occurrence MUST oppose the absence criterion only when its closed true-time range is wholly inside the half-open history.

<a id="qual-req-019"></a>
## QUAL-REQ-019 Occurrence compatibility

Accepted aliases and matching occurrence identities MUST share a common time within their declared bounds, with an empty intersection reported incompatible.
