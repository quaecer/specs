# Transfer requirements

The capitalized key word MUST follows [BCP 14](https://www.rfc-editor.org/info/bcp14).

<a id="qual-req-031"></a>
## QUAL-REQ-031 Transfer bindings

A transfer MUST bind source and target to one exact accepted view and traverse the reflexive-transitive order from target kind to source kind.

<a id="qual-req-032"></a>
## QUAL-REQ-032 Identical criterion instance

A carried finding MUST have equal canonical criterion semantics and be applicable and definite under compatible, consistent source and target views.

<a id="qual-req-033"></a>
## QUAL-REQ-033 Target recomputation

A transfer MUST retain exact source-finding and criterion digests, preserve directly assessed target criteria and recompute the target prerequisite result without transferring authorization.

<a id="qual-req-034"></a>
## QUAL-REQ-034 Receiving transfer

A receiver MUST recompute the transfer and its exact bindings from both raw requests, including when they are carried in the fixed RDF frame.
