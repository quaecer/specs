# Committed coverage requirements

The capitalized key word MUST follows [BCP 14](https://www.rfc-editor.org/info/bcp14).

<a id="qual-req-022"></a>
## QUAL-REQ-022 Inventory commitment

A selected capture MUST bind the canonical ordered inventory of every committed unit and its exact partition before evaluation.

<a id="qual-req-023"></a>
## QUAL-REQ-023 Certain capture

Coverage MUST merge nominal scopes on the same clock before subtracting its bounded error and policy margin, and MUST union different clocks only after erosion.

<a id="qual-req-024"></a>
## QUAL-REQ-024 Witnessed omission

An accepted exposure MUST defeat the eligible candidate units when its closed range is entirely within their certain union, with typed units and a separately evaluated forbidden exposure.

<a id="qual-req-025"></a>
## QUAL-REQ-025 Observer-relative status

An overlapping, opposing capture status MUST defeat a complete unit only for the same observer, on a shared channel and within their certain overlap.
